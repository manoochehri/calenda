## Putla carnival versus the Oaxaca city calenda
The tiliche is documented against the Putla carnival, a different event from the Guelaguetza convite
and city calendas where chinas appear. Keep them labelled as distinct. The tiliche entry stays an
inference until the owner confirms it, and no in-game text may state a community, festival or
tradition for it (spec s10, art-brief-level-1 attribution note) until the owner confirms the specific
attribution.

## Design rules for art
- Original primitives; no copied photo or art; no guessed Roblox asset ID (mark UNVERIFIED).
- Each roster type reads at phone size by silhouette plus one prop: China = tall basket on the head and wide skirt; trumpet = bell silhouette; tuba = large curve; drum = round body on the front; firework carrier = stick with a spark head; tiliche = patchwork cloth layers; rooster = comb and tail; vaca = four-legged body.
- Player-facing names are Spanish and live in `Strings`, never in generic run state.

## What is not claimed
Cultural authenticity, community attribution, local rooster and vaca forms, and whether the cast
reads well in Studio or on a phone are UNVERIFIED. Headless tests cannot show them.
