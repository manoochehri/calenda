# Level 1 background and HUD Studio checklist

The Level 1 background has been built from native Roblox geometry. Its appearance and cost in
Roblox Studio are **UNVERIFIED** until this checklist is completed on the target device.

## Background appearance

- [ ] Run from the opening plaza through the street and into the finale plaza. The ground plate
      fills the view around the route; there are no empty checkerboard edges or visible seams.
- [ ] Distant tree rows and hills frame the street and continue behind the distant buildings and
      plaza. They read as depth, not as obstacles or another playable lane.
- [ ] Low-rise town rows fill the wide ground band between the street façades and the distant
      hills on both sides, from the opening plaza through the finale, without entering the play
      corridor or leaving obvious gaps at phone landscape width.
- [ ] The scenery stays subdued. The road, gates, lead, parade and warnings remain the clearest
      elements throughout the run.
- [ ] Check the view at 16:9, 4:3 and 19.5:9 landscape. No background geometry enters the road or
      camera corridor, obscures upcoming gates, or clips into foreground buildings.
- [ ] Reset and start again. The background appears once, in the same arrangement, without
      duplicate ground plates, trees, hills or atmosphere.

## HUD readability and performance

- [ ] Confirm the HUD remains readable over the filled background from the start through both
      plazas, at each supported landscape aspect ratio.
- [ ] Check that scenery does not cover HUD text or warnings as the camera moves and the parade
      grows.
- [ ] Observe the street while its scenery and dressing appear. Record the device and any visible
      hitch or frame-rate drop; no Studio performance measurement is recorded by this checklist.

Record the Studio version, device, aspect ratio and any issues found when completing these checks.
