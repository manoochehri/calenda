# Level 1 art brief

## Goal

Make the first street feel like a lively, warm place while keeping the road, player, crowd,
gate symbols and incoming waves readable from the high camera on a phone in landscape.

## Visual cues from the local reference set

The owner-provided street images show a narrow route between colorful, varied facades, with
overhead papel picado leading the eye toward the distance. The crowd images show that a dense
wave should read as one clear mass, with the player’s parade visually distinct from it. The
gameplay screenshot from 2026-10-23 at 11:28 shows why the road, lead, sections and HUD need
strong separation at the actual phone view size.

These references are a written visual brief only. They are not source art: do not reproduce
people, faces, signs, shop fronts, logos, exact compositions or photo textures.

## Calenda direction

- Use a deep warm brown road, a lighter ochre sidewalk and a pale sand ground. A terracotta
  plaza marks the finish. Keep each plane distinct by both value and hue.
- Frame the street with a few simple painted facades in terracotta, amber and muted green.
  Use dark openings as broad window and doorway shapes; avoid small realistic detail.
- Hang a small number of broad, bright papel picado strips over the route. They add rhythm and
  depth while leaving the camera’s sightline to gates and the parade open.
- Keep the player’s own avatar as the lead. Its bright ground ring is the steering anchor.
  Followers use a clear blue family against the warm road; sections keep their own colorful,
  distinct silhouettes.
- Keep the top HUD grouped into short rows, with a narrow section meter. Leave the middle of
  the road open so players can read upcoming gates and see the crowd approach.
- Keep the parade joyful and friendly. Use shape, pose and text as well as color; the game must
  remain understandable with sound off.

## Studio check

Presentation is **UNVERIFIED** until checked in Roblox Studio. On a phone-sized landscape
viewport, confirm that:

- road, sidewalk and background separate clearly and the road remains the main route;
- facades and paper strips feel festive without hiding gate symbols, wave meters or the lead;
- the player avatar and ground ring are easy to find while steering;
- loose followers, all section representatives, HP/crowd totals and the section meter remain
  fully on screen and do not cover the next gate row;
- the same view remains legible with sound muted and at the narrow and wide landscape sizes
  available in Studio’s device emulator.
