# 0008: Music plays on; no in-game mute

**Date:** 2026-10-25  **Status:** accepted

## Decision
Music plays on from the start of the run. The in-game music toggle is removed. Device volume and the phone's silent switch are the mute. Issue [#134](https://github.com/manoochehri/calenda/issues/134). Owner: this is a kids' game, so a button to silence the music is not needed.
